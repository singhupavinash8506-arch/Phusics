# UP Board Class 12 Physics — question pattern analysis

Basis for building the practice sets. Drawn from the official 2026-27 syllabus and four papers:
the official model papers for 2019, 2021-22 and 2025-26 (in `../question-papers/`), plus a
third-party transcript of the **actual February 2026 board paper, Set 346** used for pattern study
only (not included as an official document).

### Chapter 1 scope — वैद्युत आवेश तथा क्षेत्र

Note that potential and capacitance belong to **chapter 2**, not here.

- Electric charge and its properties — quantisation (q = ne), conservation, additivity
- Coulomb's law in vacuum and in a medium (F = F₀/K); superposition principle
- Electric field E = F/q₀; field of a point charge; electric field lines and their properties
- Electric dipole; dipole moment p = q·2a; axial field 2kp/r³; equatorial field kp/r³
- Torque on a dipole in a uniform field, τ = pE sin θ
- Continuous charge distribution — λ, σ, ρ
- Electric flux φ = EA cos θ
- Gauss's law and its application to an infinite straight wire, an infinite plane sheet, and a
  thin spherical shell (inside and outside)
- Conductors vs insulators, free and bound charges, polarisation, electrostatic shielding

## How Electrostatics is actually asked

Evidence from the four papers:

| Paper | Question | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| Feb 2026 | Coulomb vs gravitational force when dielectric constant → ∞ (MCQ) | 1 |
| Feb 2026 | Meaning if electric field lines were to intersect | 1 |
| Feb 2026 | Total flux through a non-conducting cylinder with axis parallel to **E** | 2 |
| Feb 2026 | Earth as a spherical conductor of diameter 12,800 km — find its capacitance | 2 |
| 2025-26 | Unit of electric flux (MCQ) | 1 |
| 2025-26 | Prove that energy of a charged conductor U = ½CV² | 2 |
| 2021-22 | How can the capacitance of a capacitor be increased? | 1 |
| 2021-22 | Define electric flux and its unit; meaning of negative flux | 2 |
| 2021-22 | Define capacitance; derive parallel-plate capacitance; how to increase it | 5 |
| 2021-22 | Linear charge density; Gauss's theorem for an infinite straight wire | 5 |

**Patterns worth building on:**

1. **Definition + derivation + extension** is the standard 5-mark shape ("define capacitance, derive
   C = ε₀A/d, how can it be increased").
2. **Gauss's law applications** rotate between wire, sheet and shell — a near-certain 5-mark slot.
3. **Small numericals with clean numbers** dominate 2- and 3-mark slots (capacitance of the Earth,
   electrons per minute through a wire, induced emf).
4. **Units and dimensions** appear almost every year as 1-mark MCQ or very-short questions.
5. **Reasoning questions** ("what would intersecting field lines mean") test concepts, not algebra.
6. **Proof-type 2-markers** such as U = ½CV², U = ½ε₀E².

## Solution style to imitate

The Feb 2026 solved paper uses a format that works well and is worth copying: numbered
**Step 1 / Step 2 / …** reasoning, then a **Quick Tip** distilling the shortcut. Our solutions go
further — they also state why the method works, name the alternative approach, and say which is
faster in the exam.
