## Marks structure (from the 2026-27 syllabus)

**पूर्णांक 100** = written paper **70** + practical **30**.
Minimum pass: **23** (written) + **10** (practical) = **33**.

### खण्ड-क / Section A — 35 marks

| # | Unit | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | स्थिर विद्युतिकी — Electrostatics | 8 |
| 2 | धारा विद्युत — Current Electricity | 7 |
| 3 | धारा का चुम्बकीय प्रभाव तथा चुम्बकत्व — Magnetic Effect of Current & Magnetism | 8 |
| 4 | वैद्युत चुम्बकीय प्रेरण तथा प्रत्यावर्ती धाराएँ — EM Induction & Alternating Current | 8 |
| 5 | वैद्युत चुम्बकीय तरंगें — Electromagnetic Waves | 4 |

### खण्ड-ख / Section B — 35 marks

| # | Unit | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | प्रकाशिकी — Optics | 13 |
| 2 | द्रव्य तथा विकिरणों की द्वैत प्रकृति — Dual Nature of Matter & Radiation | 6 |
| 3 | परमाणु तथा नाभिक — Atoms & Nuclei | 8 |
| 4 | इलेक्ट्रॉनिक युक्तियाँ — Electronic Devices (qualitative only) | 8 |

Optics at 13 marks is the single heaviest unit in the whole paper.

### प्रयोगात्मक / Practical — 30 marks, 4 hours

| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| **External** — any two experiments, one from Section A + one from Section B (2 × 5) | 10 |
| **External** — viva based on the experiments | 5 |
| **Internal** — practical record | 4 |
| **Internal** — project work + viva on it | 8 |
| **Internal** — sessional work / continuous assessment | 3 |

How each 5-mark experiment is split: 1 mark each for procedural skill & correct observation ·
adequate number of observations with proper tabulation · calculation or graph ·
result/conclusion stated with correct units · diagram (circuit, ray or theoretical).

*Private (व्यक्तिगत) candidates:* instead of the record and sessional marks, project work carries 15.

## Practical experiment list (20 experiments)

**Section A — 1 to 11:** refractive index of a glass slab using a travelling microscope ·
refractive index of a liquid using a plane mirror and convex lens · focal length of a concave mirror
from *u*–*v* values · verifying Ohm's law with ammeter & voltmeter and finding specific resistance ·
focal length of a convex mirror using a convex lens · focal length of a convex lens by plotting
*u*–*v* or 1/*u*–1/*v* · focal length of a concave lens using a convex lens · angle of minimum
deviation and refractive index of a prism from an *i*–*δ* graph · resistance and specific resistance
of a wire with a metre bridge · verifying series/parallel resistance laws with a metre bridge ·
internal resistance of a cell using a voltmeter and resistance box.

**Section B — 12 to 20:** comparing EMFs of two primary cells with a potentiometer · internal
resistance of a primary cell with a potentiometer · focal length of a convex lens by the displacement
method · resistance and figure of merit of a galvanometer by the half-deflection method · converting
a galvanometer into an ammeter of desired range · converting a galvanometer into a voltmeter of
desired range · characteristic curve of a p-n diode and its forward-bias resistance · characteristic
curve of a Zener diode · reverse breakdown voltage from the Zener characteristic curve.

> In the original PDF the last few experiments are numbered 19, 18, 20 — the board printed 18 and 19
> out of order. Listed above in logical order.

## The 2025-26 paper at a glance

Time **3 hours 15 minutes** (first 15 minutes for reading the paper), max marks **70**.
It opens with खण्ड-'अ' — six 1-mark MCQs covering electric flux units, specific conductivity units,
dimensions of √(μ₀/ε₀), average AC power from given v(t) and i(t), single-slit diffraction angular
width, and the dopant for p-type germanium. Later sections are descriptive, with
*अथवा* (or) choices — e.g. deriving the axial magnetic field of a current loop from the Biot–Savart
law, or the moving-coil galvanometer; the thin-lens formula 1/f = (n−1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂), or coherent
sources with an interference/diffraction comparison.

## Note on reading the Hindi PDFs

The syllabus, the 2019 paper and the 2025-26 paper use the legacy Krutidev/Chanakya font encoding.
They **display and print perfectly**, but if you select and copy text you get gibberish like
`“kSf{kd l=&2026&27` instead of `शैक्षिक सत्र 2026-27`, and Ctrl+F in Hindi will not work. That is how
the board publishes them. The 2021-22 paper is the exception — it uses proper Unicode Hindi, so
copy and search work normally there.

Every file is byte-for-byte as published; nothing was altered. Only filenames were changed, to show
the session. Originals: `151-Physics-Class-12.pdf` (syllabus), `MODEL_PAPER_2018_19_Inter/151-PHYSICS.pdf`
(2019), `ModalPaperClass12th_202122/151-PHYSICS_H.pdf` (2021-22), `ModelPaper/class12/151-Physics.pdf`
(2025-26). Verify any file with `sha256sum -c checksums.sha256` inside `question-papers/`.
